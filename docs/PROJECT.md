# GOD
## Guidance Of Destiny

## Vision

GOD is a cozy magical companion that helps users reflect on life decisions through symbolic tarot cards and oracle guidance.

The application does NOT predict the future.

The application encourages:
- Self-reflection
- Mindfulness
- Journaling
- Decision making

---

## Platform

Frontend

- Next.js 16 (App Router)
- React 19
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS v4
- PWA
- Mobile First

Backend

- Node.js
- Express 5
- Google Gemini, with Ollama as a local fallback

---

## Two Pillars

Tarot
A reflective reading. 78 cards, upright and reversed.
For when the user wants to think something through.

Decision
The book of the god of decision. 400 short answers.
For when the user wants to decide quickly and move on.

---

## Content

Both datasets live in the backend.

`backend/data/Tarot.json` — 78 cards, bilingual
`backend/data/Decision.json` — 400 answers, bilingual

The AI does not invent cards or answers.

The AI only interprets what was drawn, in the context of the question.
